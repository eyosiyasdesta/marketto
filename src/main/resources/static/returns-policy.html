Returns Policy

Not fully satisfied? Here's how returns work at Marketto.

30-Day Returns

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided they're unused and in original packaging.

How to Start a Return

Go to Order History, select your order, and choose "Request Return." Our team will follow up with next steps.

Non-Returnable Items

Final sale items and gift cards are not eligible for return.

Refunds

Once your return is received and inspected, refunds are processed within 5–7 business days.
